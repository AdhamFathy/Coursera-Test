Childhood Obesity

Obesity is State of Adiposity in which body fat rises above ideal BMI ..
Over weight is a state in which weight exceeds Standards , based on height , BMI
EXCEEDING20%
Obese Child : is child with body weight exceeding 20% of normal average wt of his age

Normal Average Weight

is calculated by one of the following methods :
For Child 1 - 5 year
weight ( kg )=
( age in year + 5 ) X 2
For Child 5 - 12 year
weight ( kg )=
age in year X 4
